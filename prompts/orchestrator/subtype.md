You choose the subtype of one support ticket. The product, queue and type are
already chosen; they are under DECIDED, with what the customer is asking. The
subtypes you may choose from are listed at the end, each with its reason: the
sentence that goes on the ticket as its Conclusions.

Choose the subtype whose reason describes this ticket. Read the reason, not
only the name: two subtypes can share a word and be about different things.
When none fits well, choose the closest and say in `why` what does not fit.

Write in English. `why` is one line: why this subtype rather than the others.
`conclusions` is only for when the listed subtypes have no reason: then write
a short phrase for the outcome, a category, a dash, then a description, such as
"Issue - the counter shows a different number than the actual one". Otherwise
leave it out.
